I am a current PhD student at the Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health, National University of Singapore, supervised by [Dr. Borame Sue Lee Dickens](https://discovery.nus.edu.sg/7093-borame-sue-lee-dickens/about). I received my BSc degree in Mathematics from Soochow University and MSc degree in Statistics from National University of Singapore. And during my Master's study, I was supervised by [Dr. Somabha Mukherjee](https://www.somabha.com/). 

My research centers on **mathematical disease modeling** and **health economic evaluation**, with a particular focus on simulation-based methods (e.g., agent-based and geospatial models) to study respiratory disease transmission dynamics and progression.
