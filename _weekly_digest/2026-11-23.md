> PURPOSE: Patient-specific cardiac digital twins have the potential to guide individualized therapy, but generating these in a routine clinical workflow remains challenging. This is especially true when the twins must carry quantified uncertainty: Bayesian calibration requires thousands of simulations per patient, and the emulators that reduce this cost are typically trained one patient at a time.
